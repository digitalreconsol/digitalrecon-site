how an engagement works

From question to defensible report.

  1. Scope

    A short call to define the question, requirements, and constraints.

  2. Collect and analyze

    Research using publicly available information, with every source documented.

  3. Verify

    Findings are checked and confidence levels are stated.

  4. Report

    A clear, sourced report with gaps and recommended next steps.
