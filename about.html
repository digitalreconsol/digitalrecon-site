about drs

Tradecraft that works outside the classroom.

Digital Recon Solutions provides practical open-source education, research, and support for organizations that need to find, understand, and use publicly available information effectively.

who is behind drs

Practitioner-led.

Drew is the founder of Digital Recon Solutions and an open-source intelligence practitioner with more than ten years of experience. That experience spans analytic and instructional roles in support of special operations OSINT missions, and training delivered to military, law enforcement, and civilian audiences internationally.

DRS exists because tools change faster than good method does. We teach and apply approaches that hold up: documented sources, verified findings, and reporting a decision-maker can act on.
