Sanctions-exposure assessment: Beijing Micropilot

bluf

A Beijing drone-engine reseller tied to the Shahed-136 engine lineage has been designated by the UK since February 2024 but appears on no US list. Its US-designated peers were listed for the same engine family.

  1. The UK designated it for producing UAV engines found in Russian drones in Ukraine, and disqualified its directors in 2025. [1][2]

    high

  2. It advertised MD550 and MD275 engines, copies of the Limbach L550 used in the Shahed-136. [3]

    high

  3. No OFAC, BIS, or State listing found when screened directly against OFAC and the Consolidated Screening List, 4 Oct 2026. [9][10]

    high

sources [1] UK FCDO · [2] OpenSanctions · [3] Wisconsin Project · [9] OFAC · [10] Consolidated Screening List

drs/case-002.txttravel threat assessment

Travel threat assessment: COP31, Antalya, 9–20 November 2026

bluf

For most attendees, physical risk is moderate. It rises to elevated for anyone planning public protest, advocacy outside UN zones, or reporting critical of the Turkish government.

  1. The host agreement bars interference in Türkiye’s internal affairs, judged case by case. Raids before the NATO summit detained at least 209 people, and Antalya banned protests during it. [3][7][24]

    moderate

  2. Disruption from the Iran war is the likeliest cause of lost travel days. We found no reported strikes on Türkiye. [12][15]

    moderate

  3. Terrorism risk is moderate. The US advisory is Level 2 and does not restrict Antalya. [10][11]

    moderate

sources [3] Climate Home News · [7] HRW · [10] UK FCDO · [11] US State Dept · [12] CBS News · [15] AirHelp · [24] Akdeniz Manşet
gaps No Antalya-specific terrorism incident found. Host agreement text not reviewed. Read the full case file

Weekend roll-up: CENTCOM area, 3–4 October 2026

bluf

Hormuz stays contested, Iran has not yet answered the US, Yemen’s war has widened to a declared offensive, and Iraq enters its first week without coalition troops.

  1. UKMTO records three tanker attacks in the Strait of Hormuz, 2–4 Oct, plus a Red Sea incident off Yemen; JMIC reports three more on 3 Oct. No claims and no crew casualties. [13][14]

    moderate

  2. The Houthis claim strikes on Aramco sites near Riyadh and Khurais. A refinery fire was witnessed; Saudi Arabia has not stated the cause. [1][6]

    low

  3. Kataib Hezbollah suspended operations, except against aircraft, after the last US troops left Erbil. [8]

    high

sources [1] CBS News · [6] The National · [8] L’Orient Today · [13] UKMTO · [14] AP

method

Good tradecraft is easy to repeat and easy to check.

  1. Scope

    Define the question, the requirement, and the limits before any collection starts.

  2. Collect

    Gather publicly available information, with every source documented as we go.

  3. Verify

    Test sources and claims. State how confident we are, and why.

  4. Report

    Deliver clear, defensible findings with the gaps and next steps called out.
